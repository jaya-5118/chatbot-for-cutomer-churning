PAYMENT POLICY

1. Accepted Payment Methods
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. Prepaid debit cards are also accepted.

2. Payment Processing
Payments are processed securely using industry-standard encryption. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.

3. Failed Payments
If a payment fails, please verify your card details, ensure sufficient funds are available, and confirm that your billing address matches the one on file with your bank. You may also try a different payment method.

4. Authorization Holds
When you place an order, a temporary authorization hold is placed on your payment method. This hold is released once the order is confirmed or cancelled, typically within 24 to 48 hours.

5. Payment Declined
Common reasons for declined payments include insufficient funds, incorrect card information, expired cards, or bank security restrictions. Contact your bank if the issue persists.

6. Duplicate Charges
If you notice a duplicate charge, please contact our support team immediately. We will investigate and process a refund for any erroneous charges within 3 to 5 business days.

7. Currency
All prices are listed in US Dollars (USD) unless otherwise specified. International customers may see converted amounts on their bank statements based on their bank's exchange rate.

8. Invoices and Receipts
An order confirmation and receipt are emailed to you immediately after purchase. You can also download invoices from your account dashboard.

9. Payment Security
We use SSL encryption and are PCI-DSS compliant. We do not store full credit card numbers. All transactions are monitored for fraudulent activity.

10. Billing Issues
For any billing discrepancies, contact customer support with your order number and payment details. Do not share your full card number via email or chat.
