ACCOUNT HELP

1. Creating an Account
To create an account, click "Sign Up" on our website and provide your name, email address, and a password. A verification email will be sent to confirm your account.

2. Logging In
Use your registered email and password to log in. If you have trouble logging in, try resetting your password using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

3. Password Reset
To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions in the reset email. The reset link expires after 24 hours.

4. Updating Profile Information
You can update your name, email, phone number, and address from your account settings page. Changes to your email address require verification.

5. Account Security
We recommend using a strong, unique password and enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) for added security. Never share your password or account credentials with anyone.

6. Account Locked
If your account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the issue persists, contact customer support for assistance.

7. Deleting Your Account
To delete your account, go to account settings and select "Delete Account." This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All order history and saved information will be removed.

8. Managing Payment Methods
You can add, remove, or update saved payment methods from your account dashboard. For security, only the last four digits of your card are displayed.

9. Order History
Your complete order history is available in your account dashboard. You can view order details, track shipments, and download invoices from this section.

10. Communication Preferences
You can manage your email notification preferences from account settings. Choose which types of emails you want to receive, including order updates, promotions, and account alerts.
