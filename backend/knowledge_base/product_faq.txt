PRODUCT FAQ

1. How do I find the right product?
Use the search bar on our website to find products by name, category, or keyword. You can also browse by category or use filters to narrow your search by price, rating, or brand.

2. Are product images accurate?
We strive to display accurate product images. However, colors may vary slightly due to monitor settings. Product descriptions include detailed specifications for reference.

3. Can I see product reviews?
Yes, customer reviews are displayed on each product page. Reviews include ratings, written feedback, and photos from verified purchasers.

4. What if a product is out of stock?
If a product is out of stock, you can sign up for restock notifications on the product page. We will email you when the item is available again.

5. Do you offer product warranties?
Many products come with a manufacturer's warranty. Warranty details are listed on the product page under "Warranty Information." Extended warranties may be available for select items.

6. How do I compare products?
Use the "Compare" feature to view side-by-side comparisons of product specifications, prices, and customer ratings.

7. Can I request a product not listed on your website?
Yes, you can submit a product request through our "Request a Product" form. While we cannot guarantee availability, we review all requests regularly.

8. Do products come with instructions?
Most products include user manuals or instruction guides. Digital copies may also be available for download on the product page.

9. What is your product quality guarantee?
We stand behind the quality of our products. If you receive a product that does not meet quality standards, please contact customer support for a replacement or refund.

10. How do I report a defective product?
Contact customer support within 48 hours of receiving a defective product. Provide your order number and photos of the defect. We will arrange a replacement or full refund.

11. What are your product categories?
We offer products across Electronics, Home & Garden, Fashion, Sports & Outdoors, Books, Toys, Health & Beauty, and Automotive categories.

12. Do you offer gift wrapping?
Gift wrapping is available for select products at checkout for an additional $3.99. A personalized message can be included with gift-wrapped items.

13. Are your products eco-friendly?
We are committed to sustainability. Look for the "Eco-Friendly" badge on qualifying products. Our packaging is made from recycled materials whenever possible.

14. Can I buy in bulk?
Bulk purchasing is available for select items. Contact our sales team for bulk pricing and availability.

15. What brands do you carry?
We carry a wide range of national and international brands. Visit our Brands page for a complete list of available brands.
