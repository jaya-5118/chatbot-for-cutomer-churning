SHIPPING POLICY

1. Shipping Methods
We offer Standard Shipping (5 to 7 business days), Express Shipping (2 to 3 business days), and Overnight Shipping (next business day). Availability varies by location.

2. Shipping Costs
Standard Shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 incur a flat shipping fee of $5.99. Express Shipping costs $12.99 and Overnight Shipping costs $24.99 regardless of order value.

3. Order Processing
Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed after 3:00 PM EST are processed the following business day.

4. Tracking
A tracking number is emailed to you once your order has shipped. You can track your package on our website or through the carrier's tracking page.

5. Delivery Estimates
Delivery estimates are calculated from the date of shipment, not the date of order. Estimates may vary during holidays, severe weather, or carrier delays.

6. Delayed Deliveries
If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, please allow an additional 2 to 3 business days before contacting support. Carrier delays beyond our control may extend delivery times.

7. International Shipping
We ship to select international destinations. International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, and additional fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the customer.

8. Undeliverable Packages
If a package is returned to us as undeliverable due to an incorrect address or failed delivery attempts, we will contact you to arrange re-shipment. Additional shipping charges may apply.

9. Damaged in Transit
If your package arrives damaged, please contact customer support within 48 hours with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

10. Address Changes
Address changes can only be made before the order has been shipped. Once shipped, the delivery address cannot be modified.
