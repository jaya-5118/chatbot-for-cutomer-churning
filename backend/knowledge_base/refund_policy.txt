REFUND POLICY

1. Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for most products. Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable once activated or redeemed.

2. Processing Time
Refunds are typically processed within 5 to 7 business days after approval. During peak seasons or holidays, processing may take up to 10 business days.

3. Refund Methods
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Credit card refunds may take an additional 3 to 5 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank.

4. Partial Refunds
If a product has been used, opened, or is missing original packaging, a partial refund may be issued at our discretion. The partial refund amount will be determined based on the condition of the returned item.

5. Return Shipping
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect product. We recommend using a trackable shipping service for returns.

6. Defective Products
For defective or damaged products, a full refund or replacement will be provided at no additional cost. Please contact customer support within 48 hours of receiving a defective product and provide photos of the damage.

7. Subscription Refunds
Monthly subscription charges are non-refundable once the billing cycle has started. Annual subscriptions may be eligible for a prorated refund within the first 14 days.

8. How to Request a Refund
To request a refund, contact our customer support team with your order number, reason for the return, and any supporting documentation. You can reach us via chat, email, or phone.

9. Exceptions
Certain categories such as perishable goods, intimate apparel, and hazardous materials are not eligible for refund. Custom or personalized items cannot be returned unless defective.

10. Escalation
If your refund has not been processed within the expected time frame, please contact customer support for a status update. You may also request escalation to a supervisor if the issue remains unresolved.
