CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Order Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement, provided the order has not yet entered the processing or shipping stage.

2. How to Cancel
To cancel an order, go to your account dashboard and select the order you wish to cancel. Alternatively, contact customer support with your order number.

3. Cancellation After Processing
Once an order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You may initiate a return after receiving the product.

4. Subscription Cancellation
You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You will continue to have access to subscription benefits until the cycle ends.

5. Refund on Cancellation
If an order is successfully cancelled before processing, a full refund will be issued within 3 to 5 business days. If the order has already been charged, the refund will be processed to the original payment method.

6. Auto-Renewal Cancellation
To stop auto-renewal of a subscription, cancel at least 24 hours before the next billing date. Cancellations made after the billing date will apply to the following cycle.

7. Partial Cancellation
If an order contains multiple items, individual items may be cancelled if they have not yet been processed. Contact customer support for assistance with partial cancellations.

8. Cancellation Fees
We do not charge cancellation fees for standard orders. Premium or customized orders may be subject to a restocking fee if cancelled after production has begun.

9. Event and Service Cancellations
For event bookings or service appointments, cancellations must be made at least 48 hours in advance for a full refund. Late cancellations may be subject to a 50% fee.

10. Non-Cancellable Items
Digital downloads, personalized items, and gift cards are non-cancellable once purchased or activated.
